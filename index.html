JUST FOR YOU

My favorite person,

I wish I could put into words what you mean to me. Sometimes I have a thousand things I want to tell you, and somehow the moment I try, every sentence feels too small. So let me start with the simplest truth: having you in my life makes the world feel softer, brighter, and a little more like home.

I don't want you to think you have to be perfect with me. I want the real you: the sleepy conversations, the random stories, the laughter that catches us by surprise, the quiet moments when we don't need to say anything at all, and even the days when life feels a little heavy. I want to be someone you can be completely yourself around.

There is a moment in Good Will Hunting when Sean talks about his wife. What makes their love beautiful isn't that she was perfect. It's the tiny details he remembers, the funny habits, the little imperfections that made her uniquely her. It reminds me that the best kind of love isn't about finding a flawless person. It's about knowing someone deeply, noticing the little things, and choosing them with your whole heart.

That's the kind of love I want to give you. I want to learn every little thing that makes you smile. I want to celebrate your victories, hold your hand through the difficult days, listen when you need to talk, and make ordinary moments feel special just because we're sharing them. I can't promise that I'll always know exactly what to do, but I can promise that I'll keep trying, keep listening, and keep choosing you.

And if you ever wonder how much you mean to me, remember this: you don't have to do anything extraordinary to be loved by me. You being you is already more than enough. I don't know what to do with all the love I have for you sometimes. I only know that I want to spend a lifetime finding little ways to show it.

With all my heart, always yours ♡
